# MediCheck Summary for KHU Digital Health

MediCheck is a synthetic EHR-based digital health AI prototype that combines three themes relevant to the Kyung Hee Digital Health Center and Digital Health Department:

- medical data science with patient-level EHR feature engineering
- explainable AI for chronic disease risk analysis
- data quality assessment for trustworthy digital health analytics

The prototype loads Synthea-style CSV data, builds a patient feature table, predicts cardiovascular disease risk, evaluates data quality, and exposes results through both a FastAPI inference API and a Streamlit dashboard.

This framing supports future expansion toward digital health platforms, clinical data standardization, and medical LLM/RAG-assisted analytics without overextending the first portfolio version.
